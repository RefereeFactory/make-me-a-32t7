How it works

  1. Write freely. Add a title and your note, pick a colour that fits the mood, then press Keep note.
  2. It remembers. Every note is saved to Keepsake’s permanent cloud store the instant you keep or edit it — watch the little dot turn green.
  3. Pin what matters. Tap the pin on any note to float it to the top of your collection.
  4. Find anything. Use the search box to filter by title or text as you type.
  5. Come back anytime. Open Keepsake on any device and your notes load straight from the cloud — nothing is lost.
